How the model works

Each evening it takes the US stocks ranked 151st to 3,000th by dollar trading volume, scores them on a mix of earnings surprise, free-cash-flow yield and six quality/value measures, skips financially weak companies (Altman Z below 1.81), buys a new name only within 42 trading days of its last earnings filing, and holds the top 45: at most 11 from one sector, sized by the square root of company size.

How much goes into stocks is set by market conditions: a 15% volatility target (10-day), halved when the market's 12-month return is negative, halved when more NYSE stocks hit new lows than new highs over 5 days, halved when the NYSE advance/decline line is below its 100-day average, and halved when the equity put/call ratio is in the lowest 5% of its past year (complacency). The rest goes into IEF (7-10 year Treasuries) and GLD (gold).

Orders are placed after the close and fill at the next day's open. This is a research project, not investment advice.
